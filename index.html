    Manifesto

    Volume

    Space is the first material. We shape the volume before we shape the surface, so the form carries the idea on its own.

    Void

    What is left empty carries as much weight as what is built. Voids give a building its pause, its depth and its scale.

    Light Choreography

    Light moves through a day and a year. We set the openings so each hour changes the room.
